The pattern
The model is rarely the problem

Why generic AI stalls in companies

Chat tools usually work. They stall on four things that have nothing to do with the model, because they know nothing about the company they are supposed to work for.

Missing

Context

No access to your price lists, rules, past cases and internal limits, so the model quietly substitutes general knowledge and the output looks finished when it is not.

Missing

Boundaries

No defined trigger, inputs or end point. Every attempt starts from a blank prompt, so nothing accumulates and nothing becomes repeatable.

Missing

Ownership

No named person who reviews the result and stands behind it, so nobody dares to use it for a real decision.

Missing

Measurement

No baseline for what the process costs today. Without that number, "it feels faster" is all anyone can ever say.

Personal productivity is not a business outcome. A chat window that helps one person draft faster changes nothing about how the process runs, who approves its result, or what it costs the company.

How the result is produced
Every step is inspectable

A controlled path from inputs to an accepted result

The result is delivered through a specialised AI system, but the system is the mechanism, not the promise. This is the path every result travels.

Step 1
Approved inputsOnly sources you have cleared, versioned and permitted.
Step 2
AI draftingThe system assembles a draft from those sources, and nothing else.
Step 3
Deterministic checksStructure, versions, calculations and claim–source coverage are validated automatically.
Step 4
Critic reviewA separate critical pass hunts for gaps, assumptions and unsupported claims.
Step 5
Human approvalYour named reviewer accepts or rejects. This step is never skipped.
Step 6
Accepted resultOnly an accepted result moves on to a customer, a ledger or a contract.
Step 7
MeasurementTime, corrections and review effort are compared against the baseline.
The rule we apply:if the consequence of an error is financial, legal or contractual, a human confirms that point. Everything else can be prepared in advance.
